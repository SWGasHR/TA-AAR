TA AAR Meeting Guide

A focused, forward-looking conversation to reflect on recent Talent Acquisition hiring initiative changes and turn today’s discussion into clear actions.

01

Purpose of today’s session

Reflect on what was expected, what actually happened, what worked, what created friction, and what we should sustain, refine, or adjust for future hiring cycles. This is improvement-focused, not evaluative of individuals.

AlignBuild one shared understanding of what changed and why.
LearnCapture practical lessons from the people closest to the work.
ActLeave with owners, decisions, and a leadership-ready path forward.
Meeting tone: candid, respectful, specific, and solution-oriented. The goal is not to re-litigate the work; the goal is to improve the next hiring cycle.

How we will work
